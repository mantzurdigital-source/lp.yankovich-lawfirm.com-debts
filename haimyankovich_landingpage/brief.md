SERVICE — SCOPE OF THIS LANDING PAGE

This landing page focuses specifically on the debt-resolution funnel (matches the client's existing "יוצאים מהחוב" guide and the workspace's debt-law focus), not the firm's full multi-practice scope (family law, immigration, traffic, corporate — those stay on the main site).

Three real resolution paths, taken from the client's own guide content:

1. מסלול 1 — צו תשלומים: ניהול החוב במסגרת ההוצאה לפועל, לפי יכולת ההחזר החודשית.
2. מסלול 2 — הסדר חוב: משא ומתן ישיר מול הנושים, להפחתת קרן החוב או לפריסה נוחה יותר.

This 3-path comparison is the page's core differentiator and educational anchor.

TARGET AUDIENCE

Individuals in Israel dealing with unresolved debt, execution proceedings (הוצאה לפועל) or creditor pressure, uncertain which legal path applies to their situation.

Lead form (name, phone, short note about their situation) + direct phone/WhatsApp contact. Static informational page — no interactive diagnostic quiz (decided against it for v1 to keep build scope tight; revisit later if this converts well, same way Lior Caspi's page evolved).
